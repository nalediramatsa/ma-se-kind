Our Story

Dumela..Hello..Awe, ma se kind (my mother's child)!

A phrase that feels like home. A familiar greeting. A reminder of where we come from, what we carry, and what we might make of it all.

Ma Se Kind is an independent creative initiative rooted in Heidedal, Bloemfontein, with an interest in the people, ideas, businesses and everyday things that make a place what it is.

We work alongside independent businesses and emerging ventures, bringing together creative thinking, design and strategy to help them find their footing, express who they are and grow into what they could be. We care about the things happening around us, the people doing things differently, the ideas that deserve room to grow, and the businesses with something of their own to offer.

But we're not only here to help builld other people's ideas. We're interested in making things of our own too. From products and creative projects, to ventures that may take forms we haven't yet imagined, Ma Se Kind gives these pursuits a shared home.

Different interests, different expression, one growing archive of things made, imagined and brought to life.

Rooted here, open to elsewhere. We are MA SE KIND.
